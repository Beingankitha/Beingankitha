## About Me

I am an **AI & Digital Twin Systems Engineer (KTP Associate)** at **Richard Lang & Sons Ltd**, working in partnership with the **University of Derby**. My current focus is on connecting fragmented operational data, developing scalable data foundations, and building AI-assisted forecasting and digital twin workflows for better manufacturing, supply-chain, and commercial decision-making.

Previously, I worked as a **Software and Data Engineer** at the **University of Leicester**, contributing to Earth observation, remote sensing, and space-related programmes. I develop end-to-end solutions spanning data ingestion, ETL pipelines, APIs, machine-learning integration, analytics, validation, and user-facing tools.

I care about software that is:

- Modular, readable, and maintainable
- Tested, reproducible, and well documented
- Efficient with large and complex datasets
- Reliable enough to support real research and operational workflows
